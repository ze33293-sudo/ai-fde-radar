# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely AI product and Forward Deployed Engineering information for a practitioner making product, customer, integration, and deployment decisions.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Market- or practice-shaping.** A major product/platform launch, new delivery model, broad enterprise capability, material pricing/access change, or deployment evidence that changes what teams can ship.
- **7-8: High value.** A concrete capability, integration, product pattern, customer case, or FDE practice with credible evidence and clear near-term usefulness.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental product improvement, limited case study, or idea worth monitoring but not yet decision-changing.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine marketing, vague partnership language, recycled commentary, or a minor UI feature.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, unsupported claims, off-topic content, or pure promotion.

# Evaluation guidance

Score recency, source authority, novelty, industry impact, FDE relevance, practical specificity, and evidence completeness. Official announcements and first-hand customer/implementation evidence outrank media summaries. Separate demonstrated capability from aspiration. Community discussion qualifies only when it contains original artifacts, measurements, or unusually valuable operational lessons.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
