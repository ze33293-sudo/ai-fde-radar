# AI product and FDE profile

Use this profile for AI products, platforms, APIs, agent capabilities, enterprise deployments, pricing or packaging changes, go-to-market moves, customer adoption, implementation playbooks, and Forward Deployed Engineer practice.

The item should help an AI product manager or FDE decide what to build, evaluate, integrate, sell, deploy, govern, or measure. Route model architecture, training, inference, evaluation methodology, systems research, and engineering-only releases to `tech-news` unless their main importance is a product or deployment decision.

Exclude generic funding news, executive commentary without a concrete change, promotional demos without evidence, and consumer chatter without implementation value.
