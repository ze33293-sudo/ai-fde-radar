# Technology news profile

Use this profile for timely AI research and engineering developments: model architecture, training, inference, evaluation, agents, safety, data, systems, chips, developer infrastructure, and substantive open-source releases.

Typical items include substantial releases, research breakthroughs, security incidents, industry announcements, technical analyses tied to a recent event, and community discussions that add practical insight.

Prefer primary papers, official technical reports, release notes, reproducible benchmarks, and implementation evidence. Do not use this profile for general consumer product launches, finance-first market coverage, routine updates, benchmark claims without methodology, or promotional material without substantive technical evidence.
